## Use of AI

**Tool:** Claude (Anthropic), an AI assistant, used during development.

### What AI helped with
- Writing and debugging JavaScript for the web app (the 3D globe interface, heat maps, panels and matching code)
- Drafting data-processing scripts (converting NASA and partner datasets onto a common 2° grid)
- Explaining datasets and suggesting which open sources fit each measurement
- Drafting documentation and the pitch outline

### What our team did
- Chose the challenge, the concept (matching any point on Earth, the Moon and Mars), the design and the user experience
- Selected the datasets and checked their sources, units and limitations
- Decided the scoring logic and the mission presets (Human vs Rover weights)
- Tested the app, checked results against known analog sites (for example Haughton Crater), and made the final decisions on what to include and how to present it
